Site Name

Healthy-Eats

This site name represents a focus on healthy recipes and lifestyle tips. Optional domain: healthyeats.com

Site Purpose

The site provides users with nutritious recipes, diet tips, and a community forum for sharing health advice.

Scenarios

  • Scenario 1: What kind of quick healthy dinner recipes are available?
  • Scenario 2: What measures are used to check caloric values?

Colour Schema

  • Primary color: #4CAF50 – Used for headings, buttons, and accents.
  • Secondary color: #364274 – Used for background sections.

Typography

  • Font: Arial – Used for all body text.
  • Font: Roboto – Used for headings.
